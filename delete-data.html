Delete your TradeVerse data

Apna data delete karo · Contact

Option 1: inside the game (fastest)

  1. Open TradeVerse and tap Settings (top of the home screen).
  2. Tap "Mera data delete karo / Delete my data" and confirm.
  3. Your player row is removed from our sheet and the game save on your phone is erased.

Option 2: this form

What gets deleted

Your name, email, phone, age, experience and all game progress stored in our Google Sheet. Deletion requests are completed within 30 days (usually much sooner). We don't keep a copy. Nothing is kept for advertising, because there is none.
