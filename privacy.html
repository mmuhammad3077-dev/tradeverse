Privacy Policy

TradeVerse · Last updated: 16 November 2026

Short version: TradeVerse is a free trading education game that uses virtual money only. We collect a small player card (name, email, phone, age, experience) and your game progress so we can run the game and improve it. We do not sell your data, we do not show ads, and we do not share your data with brokers, prop firms or affiliate partners. You can delete your data at any time from this page or from Settings in the game.

1. Who we are

TradeVerse is made by Muhammad Mudasir (AlgoAli Trader), available at game.algoalitrader.com and as an Android app on Google Play. For any privacy question, use the contact form on the data page.

2. What we collect

  • Player card (you type it in): name, email address, phone number, age, and your trading experience level. You must tick a consent box before it is sent.
  • Game progress: stage, rank, XP, coins, number of market days, stars, badges, streak, academy status, readiness score and challenge status, plus the language you chose and the time you last played.
  • On your device only: your full game save (trades in the demo account, journal notes, settings) is stored in your browser/app storage and is not uploaded.

We do not collect payment details, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Why we use it

  • To keep a record of players and their progress (one row per email).
  • To understand which parts of the game help people learn, and to improve lessons.
  • To contact you about the game, only if needed (for example an important update). No marketing spam.

4. Where it is stored

The player card and progress are sent over HTTPS to a private Google Sheet owned by the developer, using Google Apps Script. Google processes this data as our service provider under its own terms. Only the developer can read it; reading requires a secret admin key checked on Google's servers.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not automatically share it with brokers, prop firms, affiliate partners or advertisers. If a partner feature is added in the future, it will be opt-in and you will be asked first.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you play. If you ask us to delete it, we remove your row from the sheet. Inactive records may be deleted after 24 months.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: in the game, open Settings → "Mera data delete karo / Delete my data", or use the delete data page.
  • Correct your data: Settings → "Player card badlo / Edit player card".
  • Ask a question or request a copy: use the contact form on the data page.

8. Children

TradeVerse is for players aged 13 and older. The game does not allow signup for anyone younger than 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us data, use the data page and we will delete it.

9. Not financial advice

TradeVerse is an educational simulation. All balances, profits, coins, challenges and certificates are virtual. Nothing in the game is investment advice, and it does not guarantee any real trading result, funded account or payout. Trading real money carries a high risk of loss.

10. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. The sheet is private to the developer. No system is perfectly secure, so please don't put sensitive information in free-text fields.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
